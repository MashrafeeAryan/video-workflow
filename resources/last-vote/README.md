# Last Vote

A hidden-role game about suspicion and voting, currently in closed beta. Modes include Mafia and a Blood on the Clocktower-inspired game.

## Mafia mode

Usually 6–8 players take part. Secret mafia members know their teammates. At night, they choose a victim while the Doctor and Detective protect or investigate players. During the day, everyone discusses the evidence and votes someone out.

The town wins by removing every mafia member. The mafia wins when it equals or outnumbers the town. Eliminated players stay silent in this mode. Room settings can change details such as whether deaths reveal roles.

## Clocktower-inspired mode

Each player has a distinct character ability. A Storyteller manages the game, and some effects can make information unreliable. Dead players can still talk and keep one final vote. Good generally needs to remove the Demon; evil generally wins when only two players remain. Character abilities can change the outcome.

## Game components

Secret role information, player portraits, discussion messages, night actions, nominations, votes, and alive/dead markers.

## Playing with AI

AI characters can hide roles, make false claims, accuse others, and influence votes. Keep the selected mode's rules consistent.
