# Settler Rush

A trading and building game for 2–4 players, inspired by Catan and Colonist. Players collect supplies, negotiate deals, and expand across an island board. Games usually take 10–30 minutes.

## Goal

Reach the room's victory-point target before anyone else. The default target is 10. Settlements, cities, special achievements, and hidden point cards help you win.

## How it works

- Roll two dice to decide which islands produce supplies.
- Collect timber, clay, wool, grain, and ore from islands beside your settlements.
- Trade supplies with other players, the bank, or harbours.
- Spend supplies on roads, settlements, cities, and special cards.
- Rolling a seven brings a blockade that stops an island's production and lets a player steal a supply card.

## Game components

Numbered terrain tiles, dice, supply cards, roads, settlements, cities, harbours, special cards, and a blockade marker.

## Playing with AI

AI players negotiate in plain language. Deals about future turns are not binding, so a convincing promise may lead to a betrayal.

Source: [Official game guide](https://gghuman.ai/howtoplay-settler-rush).
