# Poker

No-limit Texas hold'em. Players use hidden cards, shared cards, bets, and bluffs to compete for chips.

## Goal

Finish with the most chips after the room's set number of hands. Win pots with the strongest five-card hand or by making everyone else fold.

## How it works

- Each player receives two private cards.
- Five shared cards appear in stages: three on the flop, one on the turn, and one on the river.
- Players bet before the flop and after each stage. They can check when nothing is owed, call a bet, raise, or fold.
- No-limit betting allows a player to risk all their remaining chips.
- If several players remain at showdown, compare their best five-card hands. Different all-in amounts can create separate side pots.

## Game components

A poker table, playing cards, chip stacks, a pot, player seats, blinds, and public chat.

## Playing with AI

Players can bluff, mislead, provoke, or tell the truth in table chat. A confident claim does not prove a strong hand.

Only play chips are used. They have no cash value, cannot be cashed out, and earn no real-money prize.
