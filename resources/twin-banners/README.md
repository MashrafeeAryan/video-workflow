# Twin Banners

A turn-based team strategy game on a 16-by-16 map. You and an AI partner face two AI commanders. Each player starts with a capital and a warrior.

## Goal

Capture both enemy capitals, or finish with the higher team score when the round limit is reached. A player who loses every city is eliminated, but their partner can continue.

## How it works

- Take turns moving units, attacking, building, gathering resources, researching technology, and training troops.
- Grow cities and choose rewards when they level up.
- Explore a partly hidden map. Enemy movement outside your team's vision stays hidden.
- Coordinate through private team messages and map instructions such as attack, defend, and expand.

## Game components

A tiled map, capitals, cities, terrain, military units, resource stars, technology branches, team messages, map markers, and an action log.

## Playing with AI

Your AI partner can acknowledge or decline instructions. Technologies let teams share resources, lend units, and coordinate attacks. Spies can read or delay messages, while other abilities create forged messages and decoy units. Cipher technology helps reveal these tricks.
