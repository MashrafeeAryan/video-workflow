# The Decision — complete episode cut

[Open the complete episode in Flow](https://flow.google.com/project/758a5e22-952d-4bbd-aa92-d8d735db6a66/scene/84f36668-821f-45a4-9cf5-fddbeee1c1c9).

One full 30-second portrait scene has been generated and assembled. This is
the complete episode cut, not the five-second preview. It remains a draft
because the quality review found continuity and timing problems. No publishing
or downloading was performed.

The opening, escape/rescue, AI trade/decision and dice/board sections were
generated as four eight-second sources. The original generated ending contained
a duplicated URL and malformed tagline. A targeted generated typography correction
also failed: full-size inspection revealed malformed lettering and a website
resembling gghunan.ai. That correction was rejected, and the earlier ending was
restored. The duplicate website and malformed tagline remain known defects.

Cost: 100 credits for episode production, including the targeted ending
correction; 60 previously spent on previews; 160 total quoted credits across
completed jobs. No jobs remain pending. Three creative revisions including the
two preview corrections have been used. No further paid correction was submitted.

The model was Veo 3.1 Fast, one output per job, 720p, 9:16. The original preferred
reference used Kling 3.0. Job IDs, prompts, actual reference inputs and edit
intervals are preserved in production-progress.json and submitted prompt files.

The trade source did not follow the requested timing. Its two questions were
preserved using source intervals 0–4 and 6–8, shortening the die close-up rather
than losing the final question. The full saved scene reports 00:30:00.
