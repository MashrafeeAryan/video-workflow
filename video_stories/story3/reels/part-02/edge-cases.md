## Boy on the ground

He lies flat on his back, head and both shoulders supported by cobblestones.
Camera is directly above, looking straight down; it rises vertically without
tilting. Ground appears around both sides of his head and shoulders. Only his
eyes and breathing move. Reject standing, sitting, elbows supporting his torso,
raised shoulders, camera at standing eye level, or an upright portrait with
houses behind his head. Keep getting up in the later escape shot.

## Reference use and identity

The user likes the preview's cinematography, not its incorrect pose. Preserve
appearance/lighting where appropriate without using the upright composition as
a pose guide. Adult captain references guide style, not the boy's face or age.
Keep the boy's face, olive clothing and red scarf consistent between shots.

## Opening title: THE DECISION

Use resources/settler-rush/visual-reference/reference-04s.jpg as the layout and
lettering reference, not as a frame to copy with the old word still visible.
After running villagers, show a wide burning coastal village with drifting
embers. Center exact text THE DECISION in large ornate antique-gold capital
serif letters, with a thin gold horizontal rule and smaller subtitle
2050 — THE LAST HUMAN PORT beneath. Keep readable for seconds 3–5. No BETRAYAL
left behind, duplicate words, misspelling, modern sans-serif or unrelated logo.
Preserve the separate final black-screen website ending at seconds 28–30.

## Trade and recognition

AI brings grain; villagers provide timber. Do not reverse who offers each
resource. Show both materials and the same robot brass insignia so the boy's
recognition connects to episode 1. Do not present the AI as a proven rescuer.
The suspicion is intentional; do not invent an unrelated betrayal or dialogue.

## Die and camera continuity

One die is already resting in his hand before the throw. It does not appear
from nowhere or duplicate. Keep hand, throw and release connected. Low camera
looks up: the die rises above the lens, turns under gravity, then comes toward
it on descent. Match shape, size and direction across the sky/cloud transition.
No teleport, hovering, intersecting fingers or impossible extra cube faces.

## Board reveal

Use realistic miniature terrain, roads, settlements and materials, lit like
the cinematic village. No pixel-art switch or unrequested change of game.
The single-die transition is a story metaphor; the actual game uses two dice.
Game resources and components follow resources/settler-rush/README.md. Cannon
warfare and the boy's escape are narrative framing, not advertised mechanics.

## Timing, text and quality

All fades and title cards count inside 30 seconds. Final two seconds are black
with exact YOUR MOVE. THEIR FUTURE. and exact gghuman.ai, readable and still.
Generate the text imagery through the chosen tool, then verify actual spelling,
position and hold. Never assume eight-second generation follows a 30-second
table: each submitted prompt must name only its shot and actual duration.
Record failed/missing requirements and fix affected material within the agreed
budget/revision limits. No new generation is authorized by this document.
